OUR STORY

About Chokchai
Elephant Camp

Who we are

More Than a Camp.
It's an Adventure.

Set deep in the jungles of Chiang Mai, Chokchai Elephant Camp is where thrill-seekers and animal lovers come together. We've been creating unforgettable days for over 15 years — combining the magic of elephants with the rush of jungle adventures.

Whether you're travelling with your crew or looking for something epic to do, we've got the perfect mix of heart and adrenaline waiting for you.

Our values

We Care Deeply
About Our Elephants

Every decision we make is guided by the wellbeing of our elephants and our environment. Adventure and ethics go hand in hand here.

  • No elephant riding — ever. Our elephants roam freely.
  • Professional mahouts with decades of experience and love for their animals.
  • Nutritious, natural diets — including the vitamins you help make!
  • Regular veterinary care and health monitoring.
  • A portion of every booking goes directly to elephant welfare.
Meet our herd

More Than Animals —
Our Family

Each of our 16 elephants has a unique personality, history, and story.

Every visit to Chokchai Elephant Camp is a chance to form a real connection — not just observe from a distance.
